# Rules extract

Copied from https://www.openserv.ai/hackathon-2 on 15 November 2026.

- **Event:** SERV Hackathon, Edition 02. Online, 2 to 15 November 2026.
- **Deadline:** "Submit by November 15, 2026 at 23:59 UTC." That is 16 November 2026, 00:59 in Lagos (WAT). The build instruction asks for submission at least two hours earlier.
- **Prize pool:** $5,000 "awarded across tracks, breakdown announced at kickoff". Prize lines on the page: `track_winner x 2`, `best_overall_build`, `special_mention`.
- **Tracks:** Dolphin (agents on decentralized open-source models), Kite (agents with a verifiable identity and spending limits), Reppo (reliable agents that turn real-time feedback into measurable improvements), Open ("Bring your own idea and build it on SERV Reasoning. Any domain, any stack.").
- **Rule for every track:** "Every track needs to use SERV Reasoning."
- **Build period text:** "Build on SERV Reasoning. Real data, real decisions, auditable steps."
- **Judging:** "Judged on creativity, user readiness and revenue potential. Announced on X and here; prizes paid out after verification."

## Required to submit

1. A public code repository.
2. A demo link or video.
3. A short write-up explaining what the project does, how it uses SERV Reasoning, and which track was entered. "SERV Reasoning must be part of your build."
4. A post on X by the deadline, with its link in the submission form.

An existing project may be submitted if the write-up explains what was built during the hackathon and how SERV Reasoning powers it.

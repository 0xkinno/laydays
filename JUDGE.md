# For judges: sixty seconds, no login

Open https://laydays.vercel.app/judge for the numbered walk. The same eight steps:

1. **The finished claim.** The landing page opens on the *Orla Jansen* claim. The strip is every hour of the ship's wait, ruled.
2. **One ruling.** On the voyage page click an interval. Three rulebooks, one clause, one verdict. The dashed interval is the one the system refused to decide alone.
3. **The trace.** Model, SERV request id, rulebook version, citation check.
4. **Rule it yourself.** Pick counts, excepted or half. The clock moves and the ruling is recorded as yours.
5. **The documents.** Six of seven are ready. Attach the bills of lading.
6. **Sign and present.** Connect a wallet on Base Sepolia. The contract refuses a late claim or a missing document. "Start a fresh claim" opens a new onchain claim on a demo clock that is always day 83.
7. **Break it.** Four attacks run against the real checks, and the onchain refusals are listed with their transactions.
8. **The proof.** What was measured, and what has not been run.

Labels on screen say which people are fictional and which runs are recorded. Recorded run: 15 November 2026.

# Lane map

Entries in this edition cluster in two lanes: spending limits and procurement guards for AI agents, and screening or auditing what an agent did. Both are real, and both are crowded.

Laydays sits outside them. It is a tool for a named professional with a legal clock, in an industry with linked figures, and it uses SERV Multipath on the exact problem class the sponsor describes: contradicting rulebooks. A human signs, and the signature is the legal act.

What the strongest entries of the previous edition did well, and what we took from it: a try-it-in-a-minute path with no clone, a settled paid call, an attack surface a stranger can try, a table of onchain proofs with links, and a section that says plainly what is not built. Laydays has the first, the third, the fourth and the fifth. The second is built as a route and not yet settled.

# Court replay cases

Eight reported English judgments (five committed in `3e93ac5`, two more in `9155e73`, one more after that, each committed before it was run), each opened on caselaw.nationalarchives.gov.uk on 15 November 2026 to confirm the citation and what the court decided. Facts and clauses are paraphrased in our own words from the judgment. These files were committed before any run.

Six cases test the time bar reading (the Time-bar Clerk, then code comparing the document list). Two test a Clause Reader ruling on a notice of readiness.

**Split.** With eight verified cases there is no honest development split. Prompts were not tuned on any of them. All five are held out. A sample of eight is small and every accuracy figure is shown with its Wilson 95% interval.

**Dropped.** Cases from the seed list that could not be opened and confirmed in this environment (The Happy Day, The Johanna Oldendorff, The Kyzikos, The Agamemnon, The Abqaiq) are not used.

**Added later.** After the first five were run, a search of the National Archives site found more judgments. Of those screened, two had a clear holding that fits the task and could be paraphrased without guessing: Waterfront v Trafigura [2007] EWHC 2482 (Comm) and The Abqaiq [2011] EWCA Civ 1127. Others were screened and left out because the holding was about something else (a sale contract, a claim by charterers, or an obiter passage) or too entangled to encode honestly.

**Third round.** Trans Trade RK v Sebat [2026] EWHC 950 (Comm), found by a further search, tests the same rule as The Happy Day: an invalid notice of readiness with no waiver means laytime does not start when cargo operations begin. About 40 candidate judgments were screened across four searches. Eight cases in total had a clear holding that fits the task and could be paraphrased without guessing. The Happy Day itself could not be opened.

# Court replay cases

Five reported English judgments, each opened on caselaw.nationalarchives.gov.uk on 15 November 2026 to confirm the citation and what the court decided. Facts and clauses are paraphrased in our own words from the judgment. These files were committed before any run.

Four cases test the time bar reading (the Time-bar Clerk, then code comparing the document list). One tests a Clause Reader ruling on a notice of readiness.

**Split.** With five verified cases there is no honest development split. Prompts were not tuned on any of them. All five are held out. A sample of five is small and every accuracy figure is shown with its Wilson 95% interval.

**Dropped.** Cases from the seed list that could not be opened and confirmed in this environment (The Happy Day, The Johanna Oldendorff, The Kyzikos, The Agamemnon, The Abqaiq) are not used.
